        💡 How does this work? (Algorithm Explained)

        First, calculate what each person should pay equally. Then compare that with what each person actually paid. People who paid extra receive money, and people who paid less send money. Match people who owe money with people who should receive money until all balances become zero.

        Example with Rahul, Aman, and Priya:
        • Total Trip Expense: ₹900 (Rahul paid ₹600 for Hotel, Aman paid ₹300 for Food, Priya paid ₹0).
        • Equal Share: ₹900 ÷ 3 friends = ₹300 each.
        • Rahul: Paid ₹600 − Share ₹300 = +₹300 (receives money).
        • Aman: Paid ₹300 − Share ₹300 = ₹0 (all settled).
        • Priya: Paid ₹0 − Share ₹300 = −₹300 (owes money).
        • Greedy Match: Priya pays Rahul ₹300.00 directly. Entire trip is settled with just 1 transaction!
        What if someone pays for another person's personal shopping?

        When Rahul pays ₹500 for Priya's shopping, the app assigns 100% of the ₹500 share to Priya. Rahul receives +₹500 in paid amount, Priya incurs -₹500 in share, and Aman is untouched (₹0). The algorithm automatically pairs Priya directly with Rahul!
